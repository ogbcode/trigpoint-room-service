# Trigpoint Hotel

## Room service

- Room service runs 24 hours, every day.
- Guests receive their order within 30 to 40 minutes. Food and drinks arrive together.
- Every order gets one reference, like TPH-1004. The kitchen gets a docket for the food and the bar gets a ticket for the drinks, each with its own amount, so both keep their own record of the order.
- Notes such as "no ice" or "less pepper" go with the item they belong to.

## Paying

- Pay now: the guest gets a Paystack link and can pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. The order goes to the kitchen and bar only after the payment is confirmed.
- Charge to room: only for guests staying in the hotel. The kitchen and bar start straight away. The front desk confirms the charge and it is settled at checkout. If the front desk declines it, the guest pays by link instead.
- The hotel takes payments in naira only.

## Front desk

- Guests reach the front desk by dialling 0 from the room phone.
- The front desk handles complaints, refunds, allergy checks with the kitchen, changes to placed orders, late checkout, laundry, airport pickup and restaurant table bookings. Room service does not.
